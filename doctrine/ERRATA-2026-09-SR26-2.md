# Errata 2026-09 — SR 11-7 superseded by SR 26-2 / OCC 2026-13

**Issued:** 16 September 2026 · Wave 0 of the Cannae Legion joint upgrade map (CL-JUM-001, decision JUM-D-08)
**Applies to:** the versioned documents listed below. They are not edited; read their SR 11-7 references as this note directs.

## What changed

On 17 April 2026 the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System (SR 26-2), the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC Bulletin 2026-13) and the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FIL-15-2026) jointly issued revised Guidance on Model Risk Management. It supersedes SR 11-7 and OCC Bulletin 2011-12, and it excludes generative and agentic AI from its scope.

## How to read an SR 11-7 reference in these documents

| The reference is about | Read it as |
|---|---|
| A quantitative model: the Cato and Cato-F gates, backtests, forecasting models, parameter drift and calibration | **SR 26-2 / OCC 2026-13 (supersedes SR 11-7)** |
| An agentic component: Thifur-R, Thifur-J and Thifur-H agents, their activation gates and their evidence | **National Institute of Standards and Technology AI Risk Management Framework (NIST AI RMF) 1.0 plus Aureon doctrine** |
| Something that happened before 17 April 2026, such as the Cato v0.2.2 backtest | **Unchanged.** It was produced under SR 11-7 and stays historical. |

The Tier 1 / Tier 2 classifications are Aureon doctrine's own tiers. They carry over unchanged under whichever framework applies.
